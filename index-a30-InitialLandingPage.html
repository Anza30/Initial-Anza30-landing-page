Origin

Why we built anza30.

Through Kinnek, Built in Europe, and our own networks, we kept finding ourselves close to ambitious founders building across Spain and the US.

We were seeing strong companies early. We were sharing feedback, making introductions, and helping where our experience or network could be useful. Over time, we saw that this could actually make a difference.

But individually, we could not access, evaluate, or invest in every strong deal we were seeing.

So we built anza30: a way to invest together in the opportunities already around us.

anza30 is not a fund where people just wire money and wait. Every partner is expected to bring something to the table: capital, dealflow, and relevant experience.

The goal is simple: back great founders, create upside together, and build a serious community of founders and investors from our generation.

Anza was the first Spaniard to arrive in San Francisco.

Now we are 30 partners on the journey.
